UPPCS Prelims PYQ Analysis

UPPCS Prelims 2026 · General Studies Paper I

Previous-year question analysis, 2018–2025

All 1200 questions from the eight GS-I papers, grouped into eight subjects. Each subject follows the same six parts: topic distribution, charts, question type, difficulty, recurring and occasional topics, and repeated sub-points.

How the counts were made

Every question in the eight GS-I papers (2018–2025), 150 per paper, was read from the question-paper compilation and tagged to one subject and one topic, so the eight subjects add up to 1,200. The 2018–2024 tags for the six static subjects were checked against the IAS PCS Pathshala sectional booklets; where the two differ, the difference is one of subject boundary (for example, sustainable development is counted under Environment here, not Economy). All cells were verified against the papers, so none needed the “unclear” mark.

Time-bound questions sit under the static subject when the answer rests on that subject (a Budget figure under Economy, a Ramsar listing under Environment). Everything else about the preceding year's events — appointments, awards, summits, sports, new schemes — is under Current Affairs & Miscellaneous, together with UP state schemes and a few sociology questions.

Difficulty is scored, not judged. Points: format (direct 0; two-statement, multiple-select and pair-based 1; three-plus statements, match, sequence and assertion–reason 2), plus 1 for multiple-select or pair questions with three or more items, plus 1 for exact figures or dates in the options or listed items, plus 1 for negative framing (“not”, “incorrect”), plus 1 for long descriptive options in direct and pair questions. Easy = 0, Moderate = 1–2, Tough = 3 or more.
